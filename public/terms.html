What CodeSpend does

CodeSpend reads usage and cost reports from AI coding tools with keys you provide. It shows spend by tool, project and git branch, and it emails or posts to Slack when a project reaches 50%, 80% and 100% of its monthly budget.

  • Figures are estimates, not invoices. Vendors report usage with a delay and bill on their own cycles. Branch costs are computed at list price from Claude Code's logs.
  • It does not cap or block spending. Keep the vendor's own spend limits on.
