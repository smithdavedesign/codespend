Budget alerts for your AI coding spend

CodeSpend pulls daily spend from your AI coding tools, checks every project against a monthly budget, and alerts email and Slack at 50%, 80% and 100%, before the invoice does.

Run it free on your machine

A self-hosted Node app with no dependencies. Your vendor keys stay on your own server, encrypted.

How it works

  1. Connect a tool

    Paste a reporting key for Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI, GitHub Copilot or Cursor, and give it a project: a repo name like acme/web, or any tag.

  2. Set a monthly budget

    Per project, with an alert email and, if you like, a Slack incoming webhook.

  3. Get alerted early

    Press Sync now once. Then CodeSpend syncs every 4 minutes and alerts at 50%, 80% and 100%, once per threshold each month.

Spend per git branch

Billing APIs stop at keys and days. codespend-branches joins Claude Code's local logs with your repository's reflog and charges each call to the branch it was made on. It sends totals only, never prompts, code or paths.

One view across tools

Daily spend per tool and per project for any date range, with CSV export.

  • Claude
  • OpenAI

Your keys stay yours

Keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM on your own server and never shown again. Remove deletes a key and everything synced with it.
